Three Practice Songs

Quarter notes get one clap. Half notes get two. The little codes under the notes (like 0E) tell you which string and fret.

1 · Hot Cross Buns

Warm-up song · C D E

Climb down from high E to the B string. Say E · D · C before you play!

2 · Mary Had a Little Lamb

Classic · C D E G

You already know the tune — now find it on two strings. Watch for the high G!

3 · Rain Drop Song

Original · all six notes

Notes drip up and down the two strings — like rain on a window. Keep every drop even.

0 / 1 / 3 — open, fret 1 (pointer), fret 3 (ring)
2E / 2B — fret 2 sharp (middle finger) · F♯ or C♯
4E / 4B — fret 4 sharp (pinky) · G♯ or D♯
